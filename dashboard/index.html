How the radar scores a protocol

implied = round( TVL × multiple(category) × growth × maturity × odds )

  • multiple(category) — the median market-cap / TVL of the peers in that category, shrunk toward the global median with a prior weight of 8 protocols, used only when the category has at least 3 peers (fewer fall back to the global median) and capped at twice the global median. Peer ratios are winsorized at their 90th percentile before any median is taken.
  • growth — 1 + clamp(change_7d, −50, +100)% × 0.25. A week of growth moves the value by at most a quarter of its size.
  • maturity — 0.8 + 0.2 × min(age, 1095) / 1095. Older protocols count a little more; a protocol with no listing date is scored at 365 days.
  • odds — 0.5 + 0.5 × the blended token-record rate (half by age band, half by category): how often comparable protocols record a token at all.

What calibration buys — and what it does not

TVL alone ranks a protocol by how much money sits in it. The radar asks what the market pays for that money: it calibrates the multiple from the snapshot's own traded peers, so a dollar of TVL in Lending is worth a different multiple than a dollar in Dexs or in Risk Curators, and it adds how long the protocol has run with no token and how often its peers have tokens at all. On a 64/64 hold-out of the traded peers, raw TVL predicts market caps with a 465% median error; the calibrated model cuts that to 92% — the ranking differences among calibrated variants are inside noise, so every adjustment term is kept small and bounded, and the numbers above are the measured ones, not a claim. What calibration does not do is beat TVL on rank: the full model scores 0.438 against TVL-only's 0.458, and no tested variant beats TVL on rank either. The model is kept for its calibrated absolute scale, its category separation, its token odds and the live/pre-token split — not for a ranking edge it does not have.

What the radar does not do

It never invents a token, a launch date or an airdrop: the snapshot carries none of those, so the farm plans are actions to take on a protocol, not promises of a reward. Nothing here is financial advice.
